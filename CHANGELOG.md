# Changelog

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-02

### Added

- Five-day campaign with 25 cases drawn from 36 randomized templates, including cases that can be real or scams.
- Link untangling, phone menus, statement audits, story checks, shredded letters, fake-site comparisons and evidence inspection.
- Real-time day timers, score bonuses and penalties, and neighbors deciding alone when time runs out.
- Town savings starting at $5,000, escalating losses and game over when savings reach $0.
- Gazette endings for completed, abandoned and bankrupt runs.
- Local top-10 leaderboard, including quit and game-over tags.
- Help-desk scenery, neighbor portraits, music, sound effects and a persistent mute setting.
- Quit confirmation and a main-menu button beside Play Again.
- Seeded replay URLs and case-template validation.
- GitHub Pages hosting and CODEOWNERS.

### Changed

- Replaced the fixed case sequence with difficulty-based randomized daily selections.
- Expanded scam guidance and linked official sources.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-02

- Initial playable help desk with five cases, three investigation views and decision debriefs.
